Dragon's Insight (unlocks at 4 stars)
Savior's Waltz (unlocks at 6 stars)
Insightful Allies (unlocks at 8 stars)
Midnight Onslaught (unlocks at 10 stars)
Strategic Leader
Start of Combat: Increase Tactical Damage Dealt by +8% of 1 Ally in any lane (prioritizes the Vanguard) until the end of combat.
Habit levels:
1 (default when habit unlocked): Damage Dealt: 8%
2: Damage Dealt: 9.6%
3: Damage Dealt: 11.2%
4: Damage Dealt: 13.6%
5: Damage Dealt: 16%
Dragon's Insight
Start of Combat: Reduce your Damage Received by -4% and increase your Instinct by +5% until the end of combat.
Habit levels:
1 (default when habit unlocked): Damage Received: -4%, Instinct: 5%
2: Damage Received: -4.8%, Instinct: 6%
3: Damage Received: -5.6%, Instinct: 7%
4: Damage Received: -6.8%, Instinct: 8.5%
5: Damage Received: -8%, Instinct: 10%
Savior's Waltz
Each Round: 13% chance to apply Resistance (-10%) to yourself and 1 other Ally within adjacency for 2 round(s). Resistance reduces the target's Damage Received.
Habit levels:
1 (default when habit unlocked): Resistance Chance: 12.5%
2: Resistance Chance: 15%
3: Resistance Chance: 17.5%
4: Resistance Chance: 21.25%
5: Resistance Chance: 25%
Insightful Allies
Start of Combat: Increase Instinct by +8% (enhanced by Instinct) of 3 Allies in any lane until the end of combat.
Habit levels:
1 (default when habit unlocked): Instinct: 8%
2: Instinct: 9.6%
3: Instinct: 11.2%
4: Instinct: 13.6%
5: Instinct: 16%
Midnight Onslaught
Rounds 6-10: 24% chance to afflict Confusion on 1 Enemy within adjacency for 2 round(s). Confusion causes the target to have a 50% chance to mistake Allies for Enemies and vice versa each time they activate a Command or Habit, or launch a Basic Attack on their turn.
Habit levels:
1 (default when habit unlocked): Confusion Chance: 24%
2: Confusion Chance: 31.2%
3: Confusion Chance: 38.4%
4: Confusion Chance: 48%
5: Confusion Chance: 60%

Vhagar habits
Ancestral Shield (unlocks at 2 stars)
Battle Leader (unlocks at 4 stars)
Eclipse Cover (unlocks at 6 stars)
Blazing Onslaught (unlocks at 8 stars)
Skyward Titan (unlocks at 10 stars)
Ancestral Shield
Start of Round 1: Reduce your Physical and Tactical Damage Received by -12% for 3 rounds.
Start of Round 4: Increase your Recovery Received by +15% until the end of combat.
Habit levels:
1 (default when habit unlocked): Damage Received: -12%, Received: 15%
2: Damage Received: -14.4%, Received: 18%
3: Damage Received: -16.8%, Received: 21%
4: Damage Received: -20.4%, Received: 25.5%
5: Damage Received: -24%, Received: 30%
Battle Leader
Start of Combat: Increase Physical Damage Dealt (excluding Basic Attacks) by +12.5% of 1 Ally in any lane (prioritizes the Right Flank) until the end of combat.
Habit levels:
1 (default when habit unlocked): Damage Dealt: 12.5%
2: Damage Dealt: 15%
3: Damage Dealt: 17.5%
4: Damage Dealt: 21.25%
5: Damage Dealt: 25%
Eclipse Cover
Rounds 3-7: 18% chance to grant Advantage (+20%) to the Ally with the most troops and afflict Weakened (-20%) on the Enemy with the most troops. Each effect lasts for 2 round(s).
Advantage increases the target's Damage Dealt.
Weakened reduces the target's Damage Dealt.
Habit levels:
1 (default when habit unlocked): Advantage & Weakened Chance: 17.5%
2: Advantage & Weakened Chance: 21%
3: Advantage & Weakened Chance: 24.5%
4: Advantage & Weakened Chance: 29.8%
5: Advantage & Weakened Chance: 35%
Blazing Onslaught
Start of Round 1: Increase Fire Damage Received of 1 Enemy in any lane (prioritizes the Left Flank) and Physical Damage Received (excluding Basic Attacks) of 1 Enemy in any lane (prioritizes the Right Flank) by +18% for 3 round(s).
Habit levels:
1 (default when habit unlocked): Damage Received: 18%
2: Damage Received: 21.6%
3: Damage Received: 25.2%
4: Damage Received: 30.6%
5: Damage Received: 36%
Skyward Titan
Each Round: 30% chance to gain 1 stack of Bulwark (Max 5 Stacks) until the end of combat.
Each Stack of Bulwark increases your Strength by +5% and reduces your Physical and Tactical Damage Received by -2%.
Gain 3rd Stack of Bulwark: Deal Physical Damage (Damage Rate: +100%) to 1 Enemy in the same lane.
Habit levels:
1 (default when habit unlocked): Strength: 5%, Damage Received: -2.5%, Damage Rate: 100%
2: Strength: 6.5%, Damage Received: -3.25%, Damage Rate: 130%
3: Strength: 8%, Damage Received: -4%, Damage Rate: 160%
4: Strength: 10%, Damage Received: -5%, Damage Rate: 200%
5: Strength: 12.5%, Damage Received: -6.25%, Damage Rate: 250%

2: Damage Received: -4.2%

4: Damage Received: -5.95%

Cloak of Terror
Odd-numbered Rounds: 10% chance to afflict Overwhelm on 2 Enemies within adjacency for 2 round(s). This chance is doubled (20%) if the target is afflicted with Vulnerable.
Overwhelm prevents the target from using Active Commands and Habits on their turn.
Habit levels:
1 (default when habit unlocked): Overwhelm Chance: 10%
2: Overwhelm Chance: 13%
3: Overwhelm Chance: 16%
4: Overwhelm Chance: 20%
5: Overwhelm Chance: 25%
